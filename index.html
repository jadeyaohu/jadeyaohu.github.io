NYC Housing Lottery MatcherDemo Matches a household's income to open lotteries using NYC Open Data.

Problem. Applicants don't know which lotteries fit their income and household, so many apply blindly or pay someone.

What I built. Enter household size and income. It works out the % of Area Median Income and income band, lists lotteries with units in that band and size, and shows preferences that may apply.

What I found. On Nov 1, 2026, 25 of the 26 lotteries marked "Active" were past their deadline, so the tool uses the deadline to decide what's open.
